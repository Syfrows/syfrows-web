Building a web or mobile app is a major investment. Many startups and companies in the US, UK and Europe now work with development teams in India to build high-quality apps at a lower cost. Here is what you need to know.

## What affects app development cost

- **Features and complexity:** login, payments, real-time chat, maps, dashboards and AI features all add time.
- **Platforms:** web, iOS, Android, or cross-platform (React Native or Flutter) for both at once.
- **Design:** custom UI/UX design and prototyping before development.
- **Integrations:** payment gateways, CRMs, ERPs and third-party APIs.
- **Team location and hourly rates:** often the biggest single factor in total cost.

## Why India is a popular choice

- Large pool of experienced developers and designers
- Typically much lower hourly rates than the US or UK for comparable quality
- Strong English communication and experience with international clients
- Time-zone difference allows near round-the-clock progress

## How to hire an offshore team safely

- Ask for case studies and speak to past clients.
- Start with a paid discovery or prototype phase.
- Agree clear milestones, demos and acceptance criteria.
- Make sure you own the source code, accounts and intellectual property.
- Use shared tools (Slack, Jira, GitHub) for full visibility.

## Start with an MVP

The smartest way to control cost is to build a Minimum Viable Product: the smallest version that solves the core problem for real users. Launch it, learn from feedback, then invest in the features that matter.
