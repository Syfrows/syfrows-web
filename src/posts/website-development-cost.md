“How much does a website cost?” is one of the first questions every business asks, and the honest answer is: it depends on what the website needs to do. A simple brochure site and a lead-generating marketing site with integrations are very different projects. This guide explains what drives the price so you can budget with confidence.

## The main types of business websites

- **Template or landing-page site:** a few pages built on an existing theme. Fast and affordable, but limited in design and performance.
- **Custom marketing website:** a unique design, 8–25 pages, built for speed, SEO and lead generation, with forms connected to your CRM.
- **Advanced website or web platform:** custom functionality such as customer portals, calculators, multilingual content, e-commerce or complex integrations.

## What drives website cost

- **Number of unique page designs:** every distinct layout needs design and development time.
- **Custom design vs template:** a custom design costs more but makes you look premium and converts better.
- **Content and copywriting:** professional copy, images and video add cost but have a big effect on results.
- **Integrations:** CRM (for example HubSpot), booking tools, payment gateways, ERPs and analytics.
- **SEO and performance:** technical SEO, schema markup and fast loading need to be built in, not added later.
- **Who builds it and where:** freelancers, local agencies and offshore agencies price very differently for similar work.

## Ongoing costs to budget for

- Domain and hosting
- Maintenance, security updates and backups
- Content updates and new landing pages
- SEO and conversion improvements after launch

## How to get the most value from your budget

- Start with your goal: leads, sales, bookings or credibility.
- Prioritise the pages that bring in revenue (home, services, case studies, contact).
- Make sure tracking is in place from day one so you can measure return on investment.
- Choose a partner who can also help with SEO and ads, so the website gets traffic after launch.
