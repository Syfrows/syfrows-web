What We Do

Lead Generation & Automation for B2B Teams

Good marketing only matters if it turns into conversations with the right buyers. Syfrows builds lead-generation systems that find target accounts, start conversations and pass qualified leads straight into your CRM.

We combine targeted outreach, landing pages, CRM setup and automation, so leads are captured, followed up and tracked without manual work slipping through the cracks.

What's included

  • Targeted outreach: Ideal-customer research, contact enrichment and personalised email campaigns.
  • Landing pages & forms: Conversion-focused pages and forms connected to your CRM and analytics.
  • CRM & HubSpot setup: Pipelines, properties, forms and lead tracking set up so nothing slips through.
  • Marketing automation: Nurture sequences, lead scoring and workflows that follow up automatically.
  • Meeting booking: Booking links and reminders that turn interest into scheduled calls.
  • Analytics & attribution: Tracking from first visit to closed deal, so you know which channels bring revenue.

How we work

  • 1. Define: We agree your ideal customer, offer and qualification criteria.
  • 2. Build: We set up data, outreach, landing pages and CRM workflows.
  • 3. Launch: Campaigns go live with tracking on every step.
  • 4. Optimise: Weekly and monthly reviews to improve reply and meeting rates.

Who it's for

B2B companies with a clear offer and sales team, including manufacturers, technology providers, IT services firms and consultancies selling into the US, UK and Europe. We work with clients across the US, Canada, the UK, Europe and India, with working hours that overlap US and European time zones.

We run outreach based on legitimate interest, relevant targeting and clear opt-outs, and follow the rules of each market.

Most outreach campaigns launch within two to three weeks of kickoff.

We agree clear targets and report on them openly, but we do not promise fixed numbers, because results depend on your offer and market.
