Working Together

Built to Work Across Time Zones

Hiring a team abroad raises fair questions. Here is how we answer them.

Overlapping hours, not overnight gaps

Our day runs 10:00–19:00 IST, with the team available to 22:00 IST for client calls. That is a live overlap with UK and European mornings, and with US Eastern mornings. For US Pacific we schedule calls at the start of your day.

A shared channel and a weekly rhythm

You get a dedicated Slack or Microsoft Teams channel with the people doing the work, not an account manager relaying messages. Every week you receive a written sprint update: what shipped, what is next, and what we need from you.

Milestone contracts and NDAs

Work is scoped into milestones with a fixed price and a defined deliverable for each, so you are never paying into an open-ended retainer. We sign your NDA before any commercially sensitive material changes hands.
