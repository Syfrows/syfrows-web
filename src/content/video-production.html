What We Do

Video Production for B2B Teams

Video is the fastest way to explain what a technical product does and why it matters. Syfrows produces explainer, corporate and social videos that make complex offerings easy to understand.

We handle the whole process, from script and storyboard to animation, editing, captions and versions for each platform.

What's included

  • Explainer videos: Animated and live-action explainers that show how your product or service works.
  • Corporate & brand videos: Company overviews, culture films and leadership messages.
  • Webinar & event promos: Teasers, speaker cuts and highlight reels that drive registrations and replays.
  • Product demos: Clear walkthroughs of features and workflows for sales and onboarding.
  • Social video: Short-form cuts for LinkedIn and other platforms, with captions.
  • Motion graphics: Animated text, data and diagrams that keep viewers watching.

How we work

  • 1. Script: We write the script and key messages with your team.
  • 2. Storyboard: We plan visuals scene by scene before production starts.
  • 3. Produce: Animation, footage, voiceover, music and editing.
  • 4. Deliver: Final files in every format you need, including captioned social cuts.

Who it's for

B2B technology, SaaS, manufacturing, engineering and consulting companies that need to explain products, promote events or build their brand. We work with clients across the US, Canada, the UK, Europe and India, with working hours that overlap US and European time zones.

Frequently asked questions

How long should an explainer video be?

Most B2B explainers work best at 60 to 120 seconds. Social cuts are usually 15 to 45 seconds.

Do you need to film on site?

Not always. Many projects use animation, screen recordings and stock or existing footage, so no shoot is needed.

Can you add subtitles in other languages?

Yes. We can deliver captions and translated versions for different markets.

How long does production take?

A typical explainer takes two to four weeks from approved script to final video.
