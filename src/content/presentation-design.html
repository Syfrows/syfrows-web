What We Do

Presentation & Deck Design for B2B Teams

Your presentation is often the first serious impression a buyer or investor gets. Syfrows turns complex offerings into clear, persuasive slides that help you win meetings, deals and funding.

Every deck is delivered as a native, fully editable PowerPoint file, so your team can update numbers, swap logos and reuse slides without coming back to us.

What's included

  • Sales & pitch decks: Story-led decks that explain what you do, why it matters and why buyers should choose you.
  • Investor & board decks: Clean, data-driven slides for fundraising, board meetings and leadership updates.
  • Webinar & event slides: Presentation design for webinars, conferences, trade shows and product launches.
  • Company & capability decks: Credentials presentations that show your services, case studies and team.
  • Data visualisation: Charts, diagrams and infographics that make complex information easy to follow.
  • Templates & brand kits: Master templates so every future presentation stays on brand.

How we work

  • 1. Brief: We learn your audience, goal and the decision you need them to make.
  • 2. Story: We structure the narrative and slide outline before any design starts.
  • 3. Design: We design every slide in editable PowerPoint, using your brand.
  • 4. Refine: Revisions until it is ready to present, with speaker notes if needed.

Who it's for

B2B sales teams, founders raising capital, marketing teams running webinars and events, and companies preparing for trade shows or major pitches. We work with clients across the US, Canada, the UK, Europe and India, with working hours that overlap US and European time zones.

Frequently asked questions

Do you design in PowerPoint or Google Slides?

We design native, fully editable PowerPoint files by default and can also deliver Google Slides on request.

Can you redesign an existing deck?

Yes. Many projects start from an existing deck that we restructure and redesign.

How long does a sales deck take?

A typical 15 to 25 slide deck takes about one to two weeks, depending on content and revisions.

Do you write the content as well?

Yes. We can rewrite and tighten your messaging, not just design the slides.
