Syfrows Digital Services Agency provides digital services only. We do not sell or ship any physical products, so no shipping charges apply.

How services are delivered

  • Websites and apps are delivered by deploying them to your hosting, domain or app store account, along with access credentials and source files where agreed.
  • Designs, videos and content are delivered by email or through a shared drive link.
  • Marketing, SEO and social media services are delivered on an ongoing basis, with work carried out in your ad and social accounts and reported monthly.

Timelines

  • Work starts within 2 to 5 working days of receiving the advance payment and the information we need from you.
  • Typical timelines are 4 to 8 weeks for a website, 8 to 16 weeks for an app, and monthly cycles for retainers. The exact timeline for each project is stated in your proposal.
  • Payment confirmation and receipts are sent to your email immediately after a successful online payment.

Delays

If a delivery date changes, we will inform you by email with a revised date. Delays caused by pending client inputs or approvals extend the timeline accordingly.
