Paid Media Run Against Contribution Margin, Not Platform ROAS

Syfrows runs Google, Meta and paid social for ecommerce brands selling into the United States and United Kingdom. We optimise against what reaches your bank account after cost of goods, shipping and returns, not the number the ad platform reports about itself.

The problem with the number in your ads dashboard

Meta and Google both count a conversion as theirs when they can plausibly claim it. Run them side by side and their reported revenue will usually exceed what your store actually took. Meanwhile your real constraint is not revenue at all. It is the margin left after you have paid for the product, shipped it, and refunded the fifteen percent that came back.

So the first thing we do is stop using platform ROAS as the steering metric. We build the model that matters: contribution margin per order, blended across every channel, measured against total ad spend. From there, decisions about what to scale and what to cut become obvious rather than contested.

What we run

Google Ads

Shopping and Performance Max structured so you can see what is actually driving sales rather than one opaque campaign. Search for the terms where intent is real, brand defence priced sensibly, and a negative keyword discipline that stops you paying for traffic that was never going to buy.

Meta Ads

Prospecting and retargeting separated so the retargeting numbers stop flattering the account. Creative tested in a structured way, with enough budget per variant to learn something. Catalogue and advantage+ campaigns configured against your actual product margins, not a flat target.

Measurement that survives the privacy changes

Server-side tracking, enhanced conversions and the conversions API, so the platforms keep receiving usable signal as browser tracking continues to degrade. GA4 configured so that key events are actually marked as key events, a step that is skipped more often than you would expect.

Creative production

Static and short-form video produced in volume, because creative is the main lever left in a platform that controls targeting. We produce to a brief derived from what the data says is working, not to a mood board.

Questions buyers ask

Will you guarantee a ROAS figure?

No, and you should be wary of anyone who does. ROAS depends on your margins, your pricing, your creative and your market, several of which are outside our control. What we commit to is measurement you can trust and a weekly decision rhythm against it.

We already have an agency. What would a second opinion cost?

The paid audit. You get a written assessment of account structure, tracking integrity and wasted spend, with no obligation to move anything. Several clients have taken that document back to their existing agency and been better off for it.

Do you handle the creative, or do we?

Either. We produce static and video creative in-house, and we are equally happy running media against assets your own brand team produces, as long as there is enough volume to test with.

Do you work with brands outside the US and UK?

Yes. We work with clients in Canada, Australia, the UAE and across Europe. This page focuses on the US and UK because those are the markets where the questions about offshore teams and currency come up most.
