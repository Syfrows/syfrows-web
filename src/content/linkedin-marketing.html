What We Do

LinkedIn Marketing for B2B Teams

LinkedIn is where B2B buyers research suppliers, check credibility and decide who to talk to. Syfrows plans, writes and designs LinkedIn content that builds authority with the decision-makers your sales team needs to reach.

We run LinkedIn programmes for company pages and for founders and leaders, so both your brand and the people behind it stay visible to the right audience every week.

What's included

  • Content strategy: A 90-day plan built around your buyers, offers and sales cycle, with clear content pillars and a realistic posting cadence.
  • Post writing & design: Carousels, single images, short videos and text posts written in your brand voice and designed to stop the scroll.
  • Founder & leadership profiles: Thought-leadership posts for founders and senior leaders, so the people behind the brand are visible too.
  • Company page optimisation: Headline, about section, banners and featured content set up so visitors understand what you do in seconds.
  • Engagement & community: Comment strategy and engagement routines that put your team in front of target accounts.
  • Reporting: Monthly reporting on reach, engagement, follower growth and enquiries, tied back to pipeline.

How we work

  • 1. Audit: We review your page, competitors and existing content to find quick wins.
  • 2. Plan: We agree audiences, themes and a 90-day calendar with you.
  • 3. Create: We write, design and schedule posts, with your approval before anything goes live.
  • 4. Improve: We review results monthly and double down on what brings conversations.

Who it's for

Technology, manufacturing, electronics, engineering, SaaS, consulting and professional-services companies that sell to other businesses. We work with clients across the US, Canada, the UK, Europe and India, with working hours that overlap US and European time zones.

Frequently asked questions

How long before LinkedIn marketing shows results?

Most companies see stronger engagement within the first month. Inbound conversations and leads usually build over 60 to 90 days of consistent posting.

Do you post on our behalf?

Yes. We can schedule posts on your company page and, with permission, prepare posts for founders and leaders to publish.

Can you work with technical or niche industries?

Yes. Much of our work is for B2B technology and manufacturing companies, and we translate complex products into clear, relevant content.

Which countries do you work with?
