What We Do

App Development That Drives Growth

Syfrows builds custom web and mobile apps that solve real business problems, from customer portals and booking systems to internal dashboards and workflow tools.

We take your idea from UX design through development, testing and launch, and keep improving the app as your users and business grow.

What's included

  • Web applications: Custom web apps, dashboards, portals and SaaS products that run in any browser.
  • Mobile apps: iOS and Android apps built with cross-platform frameworks such as React Native and Flutter.
  • UI/UX design: User research, wireframes and interface design focused on ease of use.
  • Integrations & APIs: Connections to CRMs, payment gateways, ERPs and other business systems.
  • Automation & AI features: Workflow automation and AI features that save your team time.
  • Testing, launch & support: Quality testing, app store publishing, hosting and ongoing maintenance.

How we work

  • 1. Scope: We define users, features and priorities for a focused first version.
  • 2. Design: Clickable prototypes so you can test the experience before development.
  • 3. Develop: Agile development with regular demos and feedback.
  • 4. Launch & support: Deployment, app store release, monitoring and ongoing improvements.

Who it's for

Startups, SMEs and B2B companies that need a customer-facing app, an internal tool or an MVP to test a new product idea. We work with clients worldwide, including the US, Canada, the UK, Europe, Australia, the Middle East and India.

A focused first version (MVP) usually takes eight to sixteen weeks.

Yes. We usually use cross-platform frameworks so one codebase runs on both.

You do. Full source code and accounts are handed over to you.
