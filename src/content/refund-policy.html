This policy explains how cancellations and refunds work for services purchased from Syfrows Digital Services Agency.

Cancellation by the client

  • Before work starts: if you cancel before we begin work on your project, we will refund your advance in full, minus any payment gateway charges.
  • After work has started: you may cancel at any time by emailing info@syfrows.com. You will be charged for work completed up to the date of cancellation, and any balance of the amount paid will be refunded.
  • Monthly retainers (social media, SEO, performance marketing): you can cancel with 15 days' written notice. Fees already paid for a month in progress are not refundable, but no further months will be billed.

Non-refundable items

  • Advertising spend already paid to Google, Meta, LinkedIn or other platforms.
  • Third-party costs bought on your behalf, such as domains, hosting, software licences, stock assets and app store fees.
  • Work that has been delivered and approved by you.

Cancellation by Syfrows

If we are unable to deliver the agreed service, we will refund the amount paid for the undelivered part of the work.

Duplicate or incorrect payments

If you were charged twice or paid an incorrect amount, email us with your payment ID and we will refund the excess amount in full.

How refunds are processed

Approved refunds are processed within 7 working days to the original payment method. Depending on your bank, it may take a further 5 to 10 working days to reflect in your account.

How to request a refund

Email info@syfrows.com with your name, company, invoice number and payment ID. We will reply within 2 working days.
