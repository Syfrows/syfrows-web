Social Media Management & Content Creation

Rush PCB

PCB manufacturing · US & UK

The challenge

Rush PCB, a printed circuit board manufacturer, wanted to reach hardware engineers and procurement teams in the US and UK, but its social media presence was irregular and not tied to the topics engineers actually follow.

What we did

  • Researched trending PCB and electronics-manufacturing topics and mapped them to the questions engineers and buyers ask.
  • Planned and managed a consistent LinkedIn and social media content calendar across posters, carousels and short explainers.
  • Designed on-brand creatives and wrote posts in a natural, expert voice instead of generic marketing copy.
  • Delivered a growth strategy with realistic targets, plus sales content and talking points for the team.

The outcome

Rush PCB moved from ad-hoc posting to consistent, professionally managed social media aimed directly at its target buyers. The relationship has continued as a long-term content partnership.

LinkedIn Management & Website Development

US enterprise technology services firm

SAP, cloud & AI services · New Jersey, USA

89K → 200KLinkedIn followers (+125%)

The challenge

A 20+ year-old US technology services company with SAP, cloud and AI offerings needed a stronger LinkedIn presence and a website that clearly presented its services and new AI products to enterprise buyers.

What we did

  • Managed the company LinkedIn page with regular posts, poster campaigns and product launch content.
  • Produced AI-assisted corporate and product videos, including webinar promos and product explainers.
  • Restructured and rewrote website content section by section to clearly explain each service and product.
  • Supported website development and updates so new products and campaigns could go live quickly.

The outcome

The company LinkedIn page grew from about 89,000 to 200,000 followers (+125%), and the website now presents each service and product clearly to enterprise buyers. It is one of Syfrows' longest-running recurring accounts.

Lead Generation & Marketing Automation

Electronics manufacturing services (EMS) company

Contract electronics manufacturing · US market

The challenge

An EMS company wanted a predictable flow of conversations with US procurement and engineering leads, without hiring a large sales team.

What we did

  • Defined the ideal customer profile and enriched lists of PCB and EMS buyer contacts.
  • Built an automated outreach system using Apollo for data, n8n for workflows and HubSpot as the CRM.
  • Used AI to personalise each email at scale, while keeping messages short and human.
  • Set up tracking so every reply and meeting is logged in HubSpot.

The outcome

The client now has a repeatable outbound engine connected to its CRM, with personalised campaigns that can be launched and measured without manual list-building.
