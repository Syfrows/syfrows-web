LinkedIn & Content Marketing

Rush PCB

PCB manufacturing · US & UK

The challenge

Rush PCB, a printed circuit board manufacturer, wanted to reach hardware engineers and procurement teams in the US and UK, but its LinkedIn presence was irregular and not tied to the topics engineers actually follow.

What we did

  • Researched trending PCB and electronics-manufacturing topics and mapped them to the questions engineers and buyers ask.
  • Built a 90-day LinkedIn content calendar with 48 posts, planned by format (posters, carousels, short explainers) and by topic.
  • Designed on-brand poster concepts and wrote posts in a natural, expert voice instead of generic marketing copy.
  • Delivered a LinkedIn growth strategy with conservative, realistic targets, plus an account-expansion deck and call kit for the sales team.

The outcome

Rush PCB moved from ad-hoc posting to a planned, consistent programme managed remotely by Syfrows, giving its sales team content and talking points aimed directly at its target buyers. The relationship has continued as a long-term content partnership.

Branding, Video & Content Audit

US enterprise technology services firm

SAP, cloud & AI services · New Jersey, USA

The challenge

A 20+ year-old US technology services company with SAP, cloud and AI offerings had hundreds of sales slides and web pages built by different teams over many years. Logos, product names and facts were inconsistent, and new AI products needed launch content.

What we did

  • Audited about 400 slides across 12 shared folders for logo errors, spelling mistakes, flattened images, non-editable data and outdated year references, delivered as an 18-tab tracking workbook.
  • Audited the full company website and delivered section-by-section rewrite recommendations.
  • Produced a 20-concept LinkedIn poster campaign and editable, on-brand PowerPoint decks for recurring use.
  • Produced AI-assisted corporate and product videos, including webinar promos and product explainers, using a reusable brand asset library.

The outcome

The client got a clear, prioritised list of every brand and content issue, a consistent visual system for new material, and a steady pipeline of video and social content for product launches and webinars. It is now Syfrows' longest-running recurring account.

Lead Generation & Marketing Automation

Electronics manufacturing services (EMS) company

Contract electronics manufacturing · US market

The challenge

An EMS company wanted a predictable flow of conversations with US procurement and engineering leads, without hiring a large sales team.

What we did

  • Defined the ideal customer profile and enriched spreadsheets of PCB and EMS buyer contacts.
  • Built an automated outreach system using Apollo for data, n8n for workflows and HubSpot as the CRM.
  • Used AI to personalise each email at scale, while keeping messages short and human.
  • Set up tracking so every reply and meeting is logged in HubSpot, and flagged overlap risk with other accounts to keep campaigns clean.

The outcome

The client now has a repeatable outbound engine connected to its CRM, with personalised campaigns that can be launched and measured without manual list-building or copy-pasting.

Website Development & SEO

Syfrows.com

Our own website · Global

The challenge

Our own site was built on an old template with dummy content, slow pages, broken forms and weak SEO, so it was not bringing in enquiries from the international clients we want.

What we did

  • Migrated the site to Astro for fast static pages and deployed it on Netlify behind Cloudflare, with HTTPS and clean redirects.
  • Rewrote the positioning and built dedicated, SEO-ready pages for each service, plus a blog and a free-audit lead magnet.
  • Added structured data (Organization, Service, FAQ, Breadcrumb, BlogPosting), an XML sitemap and Search Console indexing.
  • Connected every form to email and HubSpot, and set up Google Analytics 4 and Tag Manager with lead, email and phone conversions.

The outcome

A fast, fully tracked website that clearly explains our services to buyers worldwide, with every enquiry captured and attributed. We use the same process for client websites.
