Hiring the wrong marketing agency costs more than its fee. You lose months, ad budget and momentum. This checklist helps you compare agencies on what actually predicts results, whether you are hiring in Pune, elsewhere in India or overseas.

Before you talk to agencies

  1. Define the goal in numbers. "More leads" is not a goal. "30 qualified demo requests a month at under ₹2,000 each" is. Agencies propose better plans when the target is clear.
  2. Know your current baseline. Note your monthly website visitors, enquiries, cost per lead and close rate. If you don't track these yet, ask agencies to set up tracking first.
  3. Set a total budget. Split it into agency fee and media spend (the money that goes to Google, Meta or LinkedIn).

Red flags to watch for

  • Guaranteed first-page rankings or "10x leads in 30 days".
  • Ad accounts created in the agency's name that you cannot take with you.
  • Reports full of reach and followers but no leads or sales.
  • Long lock-in contracts with no performance review points.
  • No questions about your sales process or what a good customer looks like.

Retainer, project or performance-based?

Monthly retainers suit ongoing work like SEO, social media and ad management. Fixed projects suit websites, apps and brand identity. Performance-based pricing sounds attractive but only works when tracking is reliable and the agency controls enough of the funnel; most combine a base fee with a bonus.

Questions to ask in the first call

  • What would you do in the first 90 days, and what results would you expect by then?
  • How will you measure success, and what will you need from us?
  • Who will work on our account each week?
  • What happens to our accounts and data if we part ways?

Frequently asked questions

How much should I pay a digital marketing agency?

Retainers vary widely with scope. Compare what is included (strategy, content, ad management, reporting) and keep ad spend separate from the agency fee so you can see both clearly.

Should I hire a local agency or one in another city or country?

Location matters less than communication, time-zone overlap and relevant experience. A local agency helps if you need in-person workshops or local SEO.

How long before I see results?

Paid ads can produce leads within weeks. SEO and content usually take 3 to 6 months to show steady growth.
